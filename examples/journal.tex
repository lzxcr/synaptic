\section{Introduction}
Readable defaults should remain predictable across machines. A useful design
system makes the relationships among title matter, argument, and supporting
metadata visible without allowing decoration to compete with content.

\begin{syntheorem}[A small result]
  If $a=b$, then $a+c=b+c$ for every compatible $c$.
\end{syntheorem}
\begin{syndefinition}
  A \emph{visual invariant} is a relation that remains recognizable across
  document modes.
\end{syndefinition}

\subsection{A measured hierarchy}
Line measure and vertical rhythm are coupled. A simple page cost is
\[
  \mathcal C = \alpha\,\lvert L-L_0\rvert
    + \beta\,\lvert R-R_0\rvert + \gamma E,
\]
where $L$ is line length, $R$ is baseline rhythm, and $E$ records exceptions.

\begin{synremark}
  Decoration is most effective when it explains an edge, an anchor, or a
  transition; otherwise it is merely additional ink.
\end{synremark}
